## Intent

The loop that sits on the Jev Atlas homepage. Silent, captions burned in, plays
forever without anyone pressing anything. It has to say what Jev's interface
actually is — a workflow breaks into narrow questions, each answered as Choice,
Score or Noul, and the answers drive ordinary code — in the atlas's own visual
language: warm paper, serif display, mono labels, one red accent.
